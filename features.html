🚨 Always Remember During a Typhoon
🏃
EVACUATE EARLY
Don't wait for mandatory orders — leave 12–24 hours before landfall
🚫
NEVER CROSS FLOODS
30cm of fast-moving water can knock an adult off their feet
📻
MONITOR PAGASA
Battery-powered radio is your lifeline when power and internet fail
🤝
CHECK NEIGHBORS
Elderly, disabled, and children are most vulnerable — assist them first
