Why We Align With SDG 13

Climate change has amplified the frequency and catastrophic energy of typhoons cutting across the Philippine Archipelago. Rising sea levels and warmer ocean surface temperatures mean storm surges reach further inland, threatening more barangays every year.

TyphoonReady PH serves as an educational bridge turning passive climate warning alerts into proactive household frameworks. By offering real-time simulators and data guides, we aim to decrease casualties and boost grassroots adaptability.

Our Framework

The Three Core Objectives

📊

Interactive and User Friendly

Traditional text bulletins can be confusing. Our interactive dashboards use graphical inputs to convey survival steps in an instant, readable format and is easy to user across different age groups.

🎒

Actionable Logistics

From strategic 72-hour go-bag packing metrics to direct localized evacuation route paths, we outline exactly what steps must be executed before landfalls.

📞

Instant Networks

Bridging communication gaps by providing verified, offline-accessible disaster lines and hotlines across national and regional rescue assets.
